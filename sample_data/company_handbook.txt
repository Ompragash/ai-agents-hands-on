ACME Corporation Employee Handbook
===================================

Company Overview
-----------------
ACME Corporation was founded in 1985 and is a leading provider of innovative technology solutions. Our headquarters is located in San Francisco, California, with additional offices in New York, Austin, and Seattle.

Mission Statement: To deliver cutting-edge technology that empowers businesses to achieve their goals.
Values: Innovation, Integrity, Collaboration, Customer Focus

Work Policies
-------------

Working Hours:
- Standard work week: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM PST
- Flexible hours available with manager approval
- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM PST (all team members should be available)

Remote Work Policy:
- Hybrid work model: 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week
- Fully remote work available for certain roles with approval
- Home office stipend: $1,000 annually for remote workers

Time Off Policies:
- Vacation Days: 20 days per year for new employees, increases to 25 days after 3 years
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Personal Days: 5 days per year
- Holidays: 12 company holidays including New Year's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas

Benefits
--------

Health Insurance:
- Medical: Company pays 90% of premium for employee, 70% for family
- Dental: Company pays 100% of premium for employee, 80% for family
- Vision: Company pays 100% of premium for employee and family

Retirement:
- 401(k) plan with 6% company match
- Vesting schedule: 25% after 1 year, 100% after 4 years
- Financial planning resources available

Professional Development:
- Annual learning budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance encouraged with manager approval
- Internal mentorship program
- Tuition reimbursement up to $5,000 per year for job-related education

Technology
----------

Equipment Policy:
- Laptop refresh cycle: Every 3 years
- Choice between MacBook Pro or Dell XPS for developers
- Monitor allowance: Up to $800 for home office setup
- Software licenses provided as needed

IT Support:
- Help desk available Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM PST
- Emergency support available 24/7 for critical issues
- Ticketing system: support.acme.com

Security Policies:
- Two-factor authentication required for all systems
- VPN required for remote access
- Regular security training mandatory
- Password policy: Minimum 12 characters, updated every 90 days

Code of Conduct
---------------

Professional Behavior:
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain confidentiality of company information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report ethical concerns to HR or management

Communication Guidelines:
- Use professional language in all business communications
- Respond to emails within 24 hours during business days
- Schedule meetings considerately across time zones
- Keep meetings productive and on-topic

Performance Management
----------------------

Review Process:
- Annual performance reviews in January
- Mid-year check-ins in July
- Quarterly goal setting and tracking
- 360-degree feedback process for leadership roles

Career Development:
- Individual development plans created annually
- Internal job posting policy: 48 hours before external posting
- Cross-functional project opportunities encouraged
- Leadership development track for high performers
